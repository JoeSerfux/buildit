Good to know

Can I change or cancel my visit?

Yes. Reply to your reminder message and we will move you to a new time, free of charge up to 24 hours before.

Is it painful?

We aim for comfortable visits. Tell us if you feel nervous and we will go slowly and explain every step.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Kids dentistry has its own calm, friendly visit, with a short check-up and plenty of encouragement.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call us and we will fit you in the same day whenever we can.

Ready for a healthier smile?

Book in about a minute. We will remind you, so you never have to remember.
